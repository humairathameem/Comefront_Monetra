Monetra — Smart Personal Finance Management

Tagline: Take Control. Spend Smarter. Build Wealth.

Monetra is a personal finance management web application developed for the CodeCanvas Hackathon — MONEYMATTERS: Make Money Make Sense. It helps users understand their spending habits, manage their salary, plan savings, track investments, and make smarter everyday financial decisions.

Project Description

Managing money can be challenging when salary, bills, daily expenses, savings, and investments are spread across different places. Monetra brings these financial activities together in one easy-to-understand application.

Its goal is to make money management smarter, simpler, and more accessible through interactive visualizations, organized financial sections, adaptive budgeting, and personalized financial guidance.

Key Features

- Financial Overview: View salary, spending, available budget, savings progress, and important financial insights.
- Salary Management: Record monthly income and allocate money according to individual financial priorities.
- Four-Part Money Allocation: Organize finances into Needs, Wants, Savings, and Investments.
- Needs Tracking: Monitor essential expenses such as rent, groceries, utilities, and transportation.
- Wants Tracking: Understand discretionary spending on shopping, entertainment, dining, and other non-essential purchases.
- Savings Management: Track savings contributions, emergency funds, and progress toward financial goals.
- Investment Tracking: Record investment allocations and contributions and review available portfolio information.
- Transaction Management: Record and categorize expenses to understand where money goes.
- Bills and Commitments: Organize recurring payments, due dates, and monthly obligations.
- Adaptive Daily Budget: Calculate a daily spending allowance and adjust future allowances when discretionary spending exceeds the plan.
- AI Money Mentor: Help users evaluate purchases, understand spending patterns, and explore ways to save money.
- Reports and Insights: Use charts and graphs to visualize spending trends, category breakdowns, and budget performance.
- Responsive Interface: Provide a clear and accessible experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Only features implemented in the submitted version should be represented as completed features.

Standout Feature: Adaptive Daily Budget

Monetra helps users understand how much they can safely spend each day after accounting for their planned commitments and financial priorities.

When recorded discretionary spending exceeds the daily allowance, the application recalculates the suggested allowance for the remaining budget period. This encourages more informed spending decisions while keeping reserved money for bills and savings separate.
