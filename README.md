About

Monetra is a personal finance web application built for the CodeCanvas Hackathon — MONEYMATTERS: Make Money Make Sense. It helps users manage their salary, track expenses, plan savings, monitor investments, and make informed financial decisions.

Key Features

- Salary Management: Organize monthly income and financial commitments.
- Four-Part Budgeting: Manage Needs, Wants, Savings, and Investments.
- Expense Tracking: Record, categorize, and analyze transactions.
- Adaptive Daily Budget: Recalculate daily spending limits after overspending.
- Bills & Commitments: Track recurring payments and due dates.
- Savings & Investments: Monitor financial goals and recorded contributions.
- AI Money Mentor: Get financial guidance based on the assistant's implemented capabilities.
- Reports & Insights: Visualize spending patterns and budget performance through charts.
